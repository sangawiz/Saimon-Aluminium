ABOUT US

Local fabrication, fitted to your building

Saimon Aluminium Tanzania fabricates and installs aluminium windows and doors for homes, offices and shops across Dar es Salaam. Every frame is measured and cut for the opening it goes into, not built to a standard size.

Based on Kitunda Rd in Asamoil, we work directly with homeowners, landlords and contractors from first measurement through to final fitting.
